01
The failure

Nothing was hidden. Nobody connected it.

Three people in three departments described the same packaging shortage over two days. Each told a different colleague. No report was filed, because nobody thought they had a whole problem — each had a fragment, and a fragment never feels worth escalating.
02
The inversion

The CEO sees the least. On purpose.

Every tool built for organizations serves the person at the top, looking down. Zharfex serves the person recording. Leadership receives a view only as a by-product of consent: claims that three non-coordinating people independently made, with no names attached and no raw notes exposed. Less information, and far more of it true.
03
The arithmetic

Corroboration you cannot manufacture.

Three reports only count as three if those people weren't talking to each other. Zharfex tracks who coordinates with whom and discounts accordingly, so the loudest voice cannot manufacture its own confirmation. A calculation, not a judgement — no part of it is a matter of opinion.
04
The limit

It cannot know what nobody said.

So it shows you its own coverage — which parts of the organization it can see and which it cannot — and leaves the judgement to you. If a week is quiet, we tell you we don't know whether that means healthy or unheard. Bounded vision, stated plainly, beats total vision claimed falsely.
